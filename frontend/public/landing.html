Polly AI

An AI debate coach that watches you argue, then tells you how to argue better.

Record a practice argument and Polly analyses your face, hands, voice and words at the same time — tracking expression and gesture live in the browser, and scoring your delivery when you stop. Nothing is uploaded; it runs on your machine.

How it works

  1. Get a topic Polly assigns a debate topic. Ask for another if you want one.
  2. Argue it Hit record and make your case. Your face and hands are tracked live as you speak.
  3. Get the report Stop, and Polly scores your delivery and says what to fix.

What it measures

  • Facial emotion — expression through the argument
  • Hands & fingers — 21 points per hand, for gesture
  • Vocal tone — pitch, energy, stability
  • Speech patterns — pace, filler words, pauses
Runs locally with ./run-local.sh — source on GitHub. Anything that could not be measured is left out of the score rather than guessed at.
