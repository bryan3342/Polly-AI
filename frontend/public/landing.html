Polly AI

An AI debate coach that watches you argue, then tells you how to argue better.

Record a practice argument and Polly analyses your face, hands, voice and words at the same time — tracking expression and gesture live in the browser, and scoring your delivery when you stop. Nothing is uploaded; it runs on your machine.
